# Drop Portal development history

The full developer changelog up to the GitHub release 1.0.0 (2026-11-12),
moved here from `CHANGELOG.md` so that the package's changelog holds only the
user-facing notes the Droppy Store shows in its Update popover. `.droppykit/`
never leaves the package (`droppykit submit` excludes it). Newer development
notes go in git history and, when they matter later, here.


## [Unreleased] (the 1.1.0 development notes, as written)

- Added Stardust: on the phone the card loosens into grains of light from left to right and blows off the edge; on the Mac a little stardust gathers at the edge while you drag, then the photo's own grains fly in, right side first, and become the card again with one short glint.
- Added Lightning: one short strike carries the card off the phone and catches it on the Mac in an ice-blue bolt. It never flashes more than three times in any second, however fast you send, and never in red.
- Made the throw one motion across both screens: the card comes out of the Mac's edge at the height it left the phone, carries on with the speed and direction of your flick and slows into its spot. A fast flick arrives sooner and can sail a little past before settling; a slow one drifts in. The light, the impact and the sound follow the card's own timing. Older phone pages and Reduce motion keep the previous landing.
- Made a landing feel physical on a Force Touch trackpad: a light tap as the card comes out of the screen edge and a firmer one as it lands. Switch it off with Haptics in Settings › Look & sound.
- Moved the card and its flight in step with the display, at 120 Hz on ProMotion screens, and drew the effects' timed motion at that rate too.
- Gave the landed card a soft shadow on the desktop and a slight lean toward the pointer while you hover it. Click and drag work as before; Reduce motion keeps it flat.
- Drew the phone page's edge light on the GPU (WebGL) while you drag and when you flick: Portal light's rift, Liquid's membrane, Wormhole's rims, Beam's lines and Paper plane's fold, in the effect's colour or Rainbow. Phones without WebGL, and Reduce motion, keep the previous look.
- Drew the crossing effects on the Mac's graphics card: Wormhole winds the photo into its rings, Liquid's drop really merges and splits with a wet rim, Portal light bends the photo at the rift in split colours, Beam glitches in scanlines with an RGB split, Paper plane's halves are lit like folded paper, and every landing throws a burst of thousands of particles. Only the card and the light are drawn: nothing reads your screen.
- Kept the previous effects for Macs without Metal, under Reduce motion (still a plain fade) and in Low Power Mode, and made Wormhole and Beam cheaper to draw there so they no longer drop frames at 120 Hz.
- Added a landing celebration to every effect: a ring of sparks in the photo's own colours (the effect's colour for documents) leaves the card's edges as it lands, the card settles without bouncing, and then it tilts like glass toward your pointer with a soft sheen, flat again when the pointer leaves. Click and drag work as before. Switch it off with Landing sparkle in Settings › Look & sound.
- Wrote the landed file's name in letter by letter, sharpening and rising into place in well under a second, with long names shortened in the middle so the extension stays. The phone's "Sent" line does the same. VoiceOver reads the full name once. Switch it off with Animated file name in Settings › Look & sound.
- Reduce motion turns both into a plain fade: no sparks, no tilt, the name simply fades in.


## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-12

- Live phone status. The Mac now knows when the phone page is really open: it counts as connected from the page's first hello on the live-preview socket and as gone about three seconds after its last socket closes. The page already closes the socket when it is hidden, locked or closed; it now pings every 10 seconds instead of 20, and a silent socket is dropped after 30 seconds instead of 60, so a phone that leaves the Wi-Fi shows as gone within half a minute. Settings › Connect your phone has a green dot and "Phone connected" while it is open, a grey one and "Not connected" otherwise. `phoneConnected` is now this live value (written by the main process, false when Drop Portal stops); "a phone has connected once", for Getting started, moved to `phoneSeen`, and an old `phoneConnected` is carried over.
- The shelf widget makes room once the phone is connected: the QR code folds into a small QR button (it shows the code again, to add another phone) next to Send clipboard, under the green status line, and the widget gets much shorter. Below them is the newest file from the phone, with its thumbnail or icon, a short name and "2 min ago": click it to open it, or drag it out as a file into any app. It is hidden until something has arrived. Until a phone is connected the widget is the QR code as before, with a grey "Not connected" line (and the newest file under it, when there is one); Send clipboard shows only while a phone page is open, the only time it can get there.
- Look & sound: the effect's picture now has a play button on it and plays the effect on this Mac when clicked, with "Preview of <effect>" under it, so it no longer reads as a screenshot. The separate Play on this Mac button is gone.

- A short tour for new people. On the phone page's first visit a small sheet walks through three steps, each with one sentence and a looping hint: Pick photos or files, Flick them off the left edge (a finger carries a card off the edge), They land on your Mac (with one line on the other way: drag to the right edge of your Mac screen). Skip, Back, Next and Done; a tap outside or Escape also closes it. It is remembered on the phone (in private mode it may show again) and the new "?" next to the title shows it any time. It follows the phone's light or dark setting, and Reduce motion keeps it still. It replaces the old one-line hint. It is remembered under a new key (`dropportal.tour.v2`), so every phone sees it once, including one that dismissed the old hint.
- Settings starts with Getting started until a phone has connected: 1 Scan the QR with your phone camera, 2 Flick a photo off the left edge, 3 Send back by dragging to the right edge (or ⌃⌥→ in Finder when the right edge is off). It goes away by itself once a phone page connects or anything has crossed, or with Hide. The shelf widget adds "Then flick a photo off the left edge." under its line (in a row: "Scan, then flick left") until then. The only new stored values are `phoneSeen`, `phoneConnected` (live, see below) and `gettingStartedHidden`.
- Settings is simpler and in the order you use it: Connect your phone (the QR code with one status line, "Ready — scan with your phone's camera", "Phone connected" or what is wrong, and the Wi-Fi tip only until a phone has been used), Send to phone (one drop area for files and folders with Choose files… in it, Send clipboard, and one line on the other ways), Look & sound (Effect, a picture of it in its colour with Play on this Mac, Color, Sound), Recent (5 rows, then Show all), Phone shortcuts (two shown, the other three behind "3 more shortcuts", each with a one-sentence tip) and More, closed by default: the switches (File Tray, links, right edge), the keyboard shortcut, the Finder Quick Action, where Smaller files lives, the Drop Portal folder and Troubleshooting (status and Reset pairing). The separate Preview effects list is gone (Preview plays the chosen effect). No setting, key or default changed.
- The Finder Quick Action sends folders too, as a .zip, like every other way of sending from the Mac: the shortcut now names each selected file or folder by its path (`X-DropPortal-Path`) and the droplet offers it as it offers Finder's selection, zipping a folder. The route still takes only loopback requests with the pairing code, and still takes the old shortcut's uploads, so a Quick Action added before keeps sending files; add it again (More › Finder Quick Action) to send folders.
- iPhone Shortcuts: four more signed shortcuts beside the share sheet's, in a small iPhone Shortcuts list on the phone page (iPhone and iPad only) and in Settings (each Add shows a QR code to scan). Send latest photo sends your newest photo or screenshot in one tap, made for the Action button, Back Tap (Settings › Accessibility › Touch) or Siri. Send clipboard sends whatever you copied: text lands on the Mac's clipboard, a link opens in the browser when Open links from your phone is on, a picture arrives as a file. Pick and send asks Photos or Files and sends everything you choose. Open Drop Portal opens the page, for the Home Screen, Lock Screen or Control Center. Each is built for this Mac (its .local name and the pairing code), signed on the Mac with `shortcuts sign` and served only with the pairing code at `/shortcut/<name>`. After Reset pairing all of them stop working: delete them on the iPhone and add them again.
- Recent: Settings has a Recent list of the last 30 transfers both ways, newest first, each with its thumbnail or icon, name, size, which way it went and when. Click one to open it; each row has Reveal in Finder and Send again (a folder is zipped afresh); Clear history empties it. The phone page shows the last 10 under Recent: tap one to download it again while it is still on the Mac, and Send again on anything that came from the Mac asks the Mac to offer it once more. Only paths are kept (in Droppy's preferences), never copies, and an entry disappears once its file is gone. The phone reads it through `/history`, with the pairing code like every other request, and can only reach files on that list.
- Folders go to the phone as a .zip: drop a folder on the right screen edge, on Send to phone in settings or on the widget, pick one with Choose files…, or select it in Finder and press the shortcut. The Mac zips it with the system's own ditto into Drop Portal's own folder (never next to the original), the status line says "Zipping …" while a big folder takes a moment, and the archive is removed ten minutes after the phone downloaded it, or after a day if it never did.
- Smaller files on the phone page: tick Smaller files under Pick (off by default, remembered on the phone) and photos are redrawn at most 2560 px on the long edge as JPEG before they leave, which sends a 5 MB photo as about 1 MB. A photo that would not get smaller, or that the browser cannot open (HEIC outside Safari), goes as it is, and so do videos. The card shows how much was saved.
- Links open on the Mac: text from the phone that is one web link (http or https, nothing else around it) now also opens in the Mac's default browser, still lands on the clipboard, and a short notice says "Opened link from your phone". The phone page says so too. Other schemes (file:, javascript:, an app's own) and links with a user name in them are only copied. Switch it off in Settings › On this Mac › Open links from your phone (on by default).
- Works on Android phones too: the wording everywhere (phone page, settings, shelf widget, banners, README) now says phone and the browser instead of iPhone and Safari, and Send to phone replaces Send to iPhone (the Finder Quick Action's name too). Add to Share Sheet stays iPhone-only and is hidden on other phones. The page links a small web app manifest (`/manifest.webmanifest`, static, no pairing code) so Add to Home screen on Android shows the name and icon and opens full screen.
- Fixed dragging files to the right screen edge: a drop let go on the portal's light now sends (the zone that takes it widens from 8 to 150 points while the light is open, where it used to miss everything but the last 8 points), and the light no longer stays stuck on screen afterwards. It goes out on the drop, the moment the drag ends elsewhere, and in any case shortly after the drag stops. The zone also takes file-name lists and file promises (Photos, Mail). Each step is a line starting with `edge:` in ~/Library/Logs/DropPortal.log.
- Files sent from the Mac while no phone page is open now wait instead of being lost: they are kept for up to 24 hours (by path, in a small list in Drop Portal's own folder, so they survive a restart of Droppy) and go out the moment the page connects, under "From your Mac". The status says "Waiting for your phone — open Drop Portal" instead of "Not sent".
- Added Drag files to the right edge to send to phone (Settings › Send from this Mac, on by default), the mirror of flicking off the phone's left edge. While you drag files anywhere on the Mac, a thin drop zone waits on the right edge of every screen; as the pointer nears it the portal opens in your effect and colour, and a drop plays the send, the file's icon falls through the edge and a short impact plays there. It takes only file drops, shows only during a file drag (noticed by a light poll of the mouse button and the drag pasteboard, no event monitor) and goes away the moment the drag ends elsewhere. Reduce motion keeps only the edge light and a fade.
- Added a keyboard shortcut, Control-Option-Right Arrow by default (change it on Droppy's Shortcuts page): select files in Finder and press it, and they go to the phone with the same edge portal. Drop Portal asks Finder for the selection with an Apple Event; if that is not allowed, a banner says where to turn it on.
- Added Add Finder Quick Action in settings: it opens a signed Send to iPhone shortcut in Shortcuts; after Add Shortcut, right-click files in Finder › Quick Actions › Send to iPhone. The shortcut hands each file to this Mac's own `POST /send-to-iphone` on 127.0.0.1 with the pairing code; the server refuses it from any other address and without the code, and keeps the copy in Drop Portal's own folder for a day. After Reset pairing, add it again.
- The phone page now slides each file from the Mac in from its left edge with a glow in the effect's colour (a short fade under Reduce motion).
- Sending from settings or the widget now says "Sent 3 files to your iPhone."

- Sending from the Mac no longer needs the notch widget: Settings › On this Mac has a Send to iPhone row above Clipboard. Choose files… picks several at once, or drop files on the row from Finder, the File Tray or the Desktop (it lights up while you hold them over it). Afterwards it says "Sent 3 files — open Drop Portal on your iPhone" (with no page open they wait for it; see above). Its tip says where the widget is: the notch's widget tab.
- The crossing effects now build up while you drag, not only at the landing. On the Mac the effect's own shape grows out of the screen edge with the card and turns into the impact as the card lands: Portal light's lens and rays widen, Wormhole's rims open from edge-on, Liquid's crown and streams bulge out, Beam's lines stream from the edge and Paper plane's paper wings spread while the photo starts folding earlier (from a quarter of the way). The iPhone's edge shows the same signature growing with the drag. Cancelling plays it backwards as the card goes back.
- The card glows much more strongly, as in the design mockups: a wide halo of the effect's colour and a bright rim round the card while it is dragged, flying, and flashing with the landing's pop. The light's own halo is wider and brighter too.
- Rainbow is no longer a still ring at the landing: its colours sweep across the whole effect while the impact plays, and turn a little with the drag before it. It costs less memory than before (two masks instead of a full colour picture).
- Every crossing effect can change colour. Next to Effect in settings, a Color row offers Electric blue, Violet, Magenta, Sunset orange, Lime, Aqua, Gold and Rainbow, plus the system colour panel for any other colour. Each effect remembers its own (Portal light electric blue, Liquid aqua, Wormhole violet, Beam magenta, Paper plane sunset orange by default), Preview plays it, and the iPhone page takes the same colour live. Rainbow changes hue with every crossing, at nearly the same moment on the phone and the Mac.
- The light is far more vivid: a white-hot core, a saturated band of the effect's colour and a wide soft halo, with a second tone across the effect and soft blooms round the card and the edge. It is painted as additive light, so it stays bright on light wallpapers too. A new colour is painted off the main thread when it is picked and cached, never on the crossing's frame.
- Fixed effects being cut off: the impact's light is sized to its full reach and halo and scaled down to fit the screen, and the edge light's panel now covers the edge's full height and the send's bloom, so nothing ends in a hard edge except the screen's right edge, where the light comes in.
- Dragging a selected card while others are selected now gathers every selected card into a stack under your finger (they fly in behind it in about 150 ms, at most three in sight, with a count badge on top). The stack moves, lights up, stretches and fades as one, leaves together on a flick and springs back to the grid on a cancel; back in the grid each card shows its own upload. An unselected card still goes alone, Reduce motion stacks without the fly-in, and the Mac's peek shows the stacked card with its count from the first touch.
- New logo, Rift Drop: a pale drop crossing a slit of blue light on a midnight tile. It is the droplet's icon in Droppy and Droppy Playground, and the phone page's Home Screen icon and favicon (the page serves it at `/apple-touch-icon.png`, without the pairing token, since it is only the logo). The Store still carries the icon its maintainer designs; this one is the concept for it.
- Each effect now has its own short, soft landing sound instead of Droppy's single drop sound: a bright chime for Portal light, a round bloop for Liquid, a low falling tone for Wormhole, two quick blips for Beam and a breath of air with a light tap for Paper plane. They are synthesised on the Mac (no audio files), play at the system volume, stay silent when "Play user interface sound effects" is off in System Settings, and also play in the settings' Previews.
- Added a Sound switch in settings, on by default.

- Added Add to Share Sheet: a link on the phone page and a QR code in settings install a Drop Portal shortcut on the iPhone, so photos, videos, files, text and links go to the Mac straight from the share sheet in Photos, Files, Safari and other apps, without opening the page. The shortcut is made for this Mac, signed on the Mac with `shortcuts sign` (it never leaves your devices), reaches the Mac by its Bonjour name so a new IP address does not break it, and is only served to a phone that has the pairing code. After Reset pairing, add it again.
- Added sending from the Mac to the iPhone: drop files on the Drop Portal shelf widget and they appear on the open phone page under "From your Mac" with a Save button (the share sheet where Safari allows it, otherwise a download; photos also show a preview you can touch and hold to save to Photos). Downloads need the pairing code, like uploads.
- Added "Send clipboard" to the shelf widget and "Send clipboard to iPhone" to settings: the Mac's copied text shows on the phone page with a Copy button.
- Added flicking several files at once: tap cards to select them, then flick one. The Mac shows a stacked card, and the landed card counts the files as they arrive one after another without crossing again.

- Rebuilt the five crossing impacts with large white-blue silhouettes and cached backing-scale glow textures: a sweeping portal wave, sculpted liquid crown, thick wormhole crescents, photo-bearing paper wings and luminous beam ribbons. Matched the phone’s left-edge forms and tuned each Mac impact’s hero capture time.

- Reworked all five crossing effects after a motion storyboard: exact timings and curves, one signature each, fewer layers and particles. The card now flies from where your finger let it go and lands on a fixed beat (0.28 to 0.32 s, Paper plane 0.56 s), then grows a little and springs back so it reads on a whole screen.
- Wormhole: three rims open in perspective on the screen edge as you drag; on landing they turn edge-on one after another and collapse into a point, with a shock ellipse and short star streaks around the file. On the phone the ring and two inner rims turn with your finger instead of spinning.
- Liquid: the photo keeps its picture under a rounded film of water, a neck stretches from the edge and snaps, and the file lands in a splash crown of drops with a squash. The phone drips 16 drops and one ripple.
- Portal light (still the default): a tall slit of light and a ribbon to the card; on landing a thin elliptical wave passes around the file and a few motes trail behind it.
- Paper plane: the photo folds in half, flies in as a plane with two contrails and a loop-de-loop, and opens back into the photo over a landing ellipse. The plane now loops on the phone too.
- Beam: the Mac rebuilds the card from nine slices of it that lock one after another, a blade of light crosses it, and nine lines pull back into the edge. The phone streams the same nine strips.
- Each landing has its own signature instead of a shared flash, bloom and shockwave; the vignette is never darker than 35%; there is no full-screen flash on the phone, no particles while you drag, no pull-back before the send, and no blur on the card.
- Made every crossing effect much bigger on both sides. On the Mac a click-through layer now covers the whole screen behind the card: a soft vignette around the right edge, a full-height wash of light and each effect's signature aimed at the card (Portal light: a tall rift with rays and sparks; Liquid: a body of water with light shafts, ripples and drips; Wormhole: a large vortex with spiral arms pulling in stars; Beam: full-height scrolling scanlines with a scanning band; Paper plane: wind across the screen and a contrail to the plane). On the send the edge flares and throws particles after the card. Where the card lands there is a flash, a bloom, a shockwave and a burst, plus Portal light's starburst, Liquid's splash and screen-wide ripples, Wormhole's spin-out, Beam's glitch, full-width scan line and light sweep across the card, and Paper plane's puff of air; it is all gone 0.9 s after the landing.
- The Mac card now has depth: it leans with its speed in perspective, lifts with a shadow, overshoots its spot after the flick and squashes and settles on landing. Paper plane is bigger and does a loop-de-loop before it unfolds into the card.
- The phone matches: a full-height rift of light with rays on the left edge, the page dims away from the edge while you drag, particles trail your finger, the card pulls back a little before it launches, the screen flashes and about 70 particles burst into the edge. Wormhole's ring and spiral arms are larger, Beam's strips jitter before they stream off with a glitch, Paper plane's plane is bigger and leaves a dashed contrail.
- Added Preview effects in settings: a Preview button for each effect, playing the full crossing (drag, flick, flight, impact, receiving, landed card). The Preview next to the Effect picker plays the full crossing too.
- Fixed the Mac card turning about its panel's corner instead of its middle in Wormhole, Liquid and Paper plane.
- Reduce motion still turns every effect into a plain fade on both sides, without the full-screen layer.
- Added an "Add received files to File Tray" setting (on by default): every received file, not clipboard text, is also added to Droppy's File Tray through the "Add to Droppy Shelf" Service once it is saved in Downloads › Drop Portal. The Service call runs in a short-lived helper process (osascript), never on Droppy's main thread, and the outcome is logged to ~/Library/Logs/DropPortal.log.
- Added a choice of crossing effects in one Effect setting, each on both sides: Portal light (the default), Liquid (the card melts into a drop that drips off the phone's edge; on the Mac a drop bulges out of the screen edge, follows the drag, then splashes and re-forms into the card), Wormhole (a spinning ring of light; the card spirals into it on the phone and spins out of it on the Mac), Beam (the card streams off the phone in scanline strips; on the Mac the strips arrive and rebuild it from the top down) and Paper plane (the card folds into a plane that glides off the phone; on the Mac a plane glides in and unfolds into the card). The Mac tells the phone page which effect is chosen, on load and live over the peek connection. A file that arrives without a peek, such as a retry, crosses with the same effect. Reduce motion turns every effect into a plain fade on both sides.
- Replaced the Landing animation setting (Fly, Fade, Pop) with Effect; an old choice reads as Portal light. A Preview button next to it plays the chosen effect on the Mac.
- Added a shelf widget with the pairing QR code, so opening the notch is enough to scan it; it follows network and pairing changes and shows a status line only when the phone cannot reach the Mac. The QR code stays in settings too.
- The peek card is never drawn past its screen's right edge, so nothing shows on a display to the right.

- Added a portal where the file crosses: on the phone a soft light grows along the left edge as you drag, the card stretches and fades into it, and a send flashes with a few sparks of light; on the Mac a thin seam of light on the right edge brightens with the drag, the card comes out of it bright and slightly soft, a send ripples along the seam and a cancel slides the card back into it. Fly landings flash the seam too. Reduce motion keeps only the fading light.
- Hardened the live peek: the card is invisible at the screen edge and fades in as it slides (no ghost on a display to the right), the peek connection uses TCP keepalive and drops after 60 s of silence (an idle page pings), Reset pairing closes open connections, only JPEG thumbnails are accepted and decoded off the main thread at up to 440 px, a still-showing landed card is dismissed when a new peek starts, the listener retries after a failure, videos on the phone page preload metadata only, and the phone's reconnect pause grows from 3 s to 30 s.
- While you drag a card on the phone, the Mac already shows what is coming: a card with the thumbnail, name and size slides in from the right edge of the screen with the pointer and follows your finger. Flick it and it waits as "Receiving…" with a progress ring, then becomes the landed card; let go without sending and it slides back out. Works over a live connection on port 8788; without it, sending works as before.
- Removed the Cloudflare relay for now: the phone reaches the Mac on the same Wi-Fi only (the relay is kept in git history, tag `relay-v1`). Gone with it: the Relay URL and Relay key settings, the relay status, the `network-client` capability and the phone page's Paste from clipboard button, which needed https.
- Simplified settings: the QR code with a same-Wi-Fi and Personal Hotspot hint, the landing animation, Open Drop Portal folder, and a Troubleshooting group with Reset pairing and a plain-words status that shows only when something is wrong. The QR code follows network changes by itself, so the Refresh button is gone.
- The phone page shows a one-time hint, says "Mac not reachable - same Wi-Fi?" on a card that cannot reach the Mac, gives up on an upload that stalls for 20 seconds (tap the card to retry) and keeps the screen awake while sending.
- Received files are quarantined, and apps, scripts and installers are shown in Finder instead of opened; long names are cut at 240 UTF-8 bytes; leftover partial files are cleaned up.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-12

- Added sending from the iPhone to the Mac with no app to install: scan the QR code and Safari opens a page served by the Mac on your Wi-Fi.
- Added flick to send: pick photos, videos or files, flick a card off the left edge of the phone, and it lands on the Mac's screen with the pointer. Click the card to open the file, or drag it straight into any app.
- Added a live preview: while you drag on the phone, the card already slides in from the right edge of the Mac's screen, follows your finger, and shows "Receiving" with a progress ring once you let go.
- Added five crossing effects, on both the phone and the Mac: Portal light, Liquid, Wormhole, Beam and Paper plane, with a Preview button for each in settings. Reduce motion turns every effect into a plain fade.
- Added sending text: paste on the phone, press Send, and it is on the Mac's clipboard with a notification.
- Added a shelf widget with the pairing QR code, so opening the notch is enough to scan it; the QR code is in settings too and follows the Mac to another network.
- Added "Add received files to File Tray", which also puts every received file in Droppy's File Tray.
- Saved received files to Downloads › Drop Portal without overwriting anything, quarantined like browser downloads; apps, scripts and installers are shown in Finder instead of opened.
- Protected every request with a pairing code from the QR code, and added Reset pairing to lock out every phone at once. Everything stays on your local network.
- Played Droppy's drop sound when a file lands, following Droppy's sound setting.
