## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-13
- New: four cartoon couriers carry your photo across. Pip the hamster stuffs it in a cheek, Tofu the cat leaps through a cat flap, Digby the mole tunnels under the screen and pops up from the bottom edge, and the Matcha Trio ducklings march it over (one duckling per file, up to five). They react while you hold the card, wait at the Mac until the file arrives, play a quicker version when you send again soon, and step aside when you click them.
- New: Stardust, where the card blows away as grains of light and gathers again on the Mac, and Lightning, one ice-blue strike that never flashes more than three times a second.
- New: a landing sparkle in the photo's own colours, then the card tilts toward your pointer like glass. The file's name writes itself in letter by letter, on the Mac and in the phone's "Sent" line. Switch either off in Settings › Look & sound.
- The throw is now one motion across both screens: the card leaves the Mac's edge at the height and speed it left the phone.
- Smoother and richer effects, drawn on the graphics card at up to 120 Hz, with a soft shadow under the landed card. Macs without Metal and Low Power Mode keep lighter versions.
- A light tap on a Force Touch trackpad as the card arrives and lands (Haptics, in Look & sound).
- Reduce motion still turns every effect into a plain fade; the couriers become a still sticker.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-12

Drop Portal's first version in the Droppy Store: flick a photo off the edge of your phone and catch it on your Mac.

- Sent photos, videos and files from the phone with no app to install: scan the QR code in the notch once, pick items and flick a card off the left edge of the phone. It lands on the Mac's screen with the pointer: click to open it, or drag it into any app.
- Showed the card on the Mac while you are still dragging on the phone, and let several cards cross in one flick.
- Added five crossing effects, on the phone and the Mac: Portal light, Liquid, Wormhole, Beam and Paper plane, each in any colour or Rainbow, with a short landing sound you can switch off. Reduce motion turns them into a plain fade.
- Sent text to the Mac's clipboard. A link from the phone can open in your browser: switch on Open links from your phone in Settings › More (off for new installs).
- Put every received file in Droppy's File Tray as well as Downloads › Drop Portal (switch it off in Settings › More).
- Sent files the other way: drag them to the right edge of the screen, press ⌃⌥→ in Finder, or right-click › Quick Actions › Send to phone. Folders are zipped. Files wait up to a day for the phone page.
- Added iPhone shortcuts (share sheet, latest photo, clipboard, pick and send), Recent transfers both ways, a short first-run tour and a shelf widget with the live phone status and the newest file.
- Kept everything on your local network: the same Wi-Fi or a Personal Hotspot, a pairing code in the QR code, and Reset pairing to lock out every phone.
- Played the landing sound off Droppy's main thread, so AirPods or AirPlay can no longer make Droppy stall.
- Kept the crossing effects off full-screen apps.
